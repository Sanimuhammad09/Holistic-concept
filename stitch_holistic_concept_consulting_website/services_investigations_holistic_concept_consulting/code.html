Frequently Addressed

Frequently Asked Legal & Procedural Questions

Can an investigation report be protected under Solicitor-Client Privilege?

expand_more
Yes. When external or in-house legal counsel retains HCC directly to conduct fact-finding for the express purpose of providing legal advice or in reasonable contemplation of litigation, the resulting findings and work product can be cloaked in privilege, subject to applicable case law.

What standard of proof does HCC apply when assessing credibility?

expand_more
We apply the civil standard: the "balance of probabilities" (more likely than not). We do not require proof beyond a reasonable doubt. We evaluate testimony against contemporaneous notes, documentary corroboration, and demeanor consistency using established jurisprudence.

What happens if an employee or respondent refuses to participate?

expand_more
While participation cannot always be physically compelled, employment contracts and institutional codes generally mandate reasonable cooperation. If a party declines, we document all notices, provide fair warning, and proceed to make factual findings based upon the available corroborating evidence.
