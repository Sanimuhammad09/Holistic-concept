## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative yet vibrant aesthetic tailored for high-stakes corporate consulting, ESG strategy, and executive advisory. The visual character merges boardroom credibility with organic vitality—reflecting the multi-leaf radial emblem from the visual reference.

### Core Philosophy
- **Authoritative & Intellectually Rigorous:** Deep slate anchors and editorial serif typography command boardroom respect, intellectual precision, and institutional trust.
- **Biophilic Vitality & Modern Momentum:** Fresh lime transitions into luminous azure, communicating growth, technological agility, and sustainable transformation.
- **Refined Materiality:** Frosted glassmorphism, whisper-soft gradients, and crisp structural borders create layered depth without decorative noise.

### Design Aesthetic
The system adopts **Corporate Modernism with Luminous Glass Accents**. It relies on structured grid discipline, generous whitespace, pristine micro-interactions, and translucent frosted overlays that soften high-density strategic frameworks.

## Colors

The palette directly extrapolates the vibrant dimensional chromatic spectrum from the floral-leaf emblem: sharp sky azures, electric lime gradients, anchored by deep executive midnight slates.

### Functional Palette Structure
- **Primary (`#0284C7` - Azure Blue):** Represents clarity, enterprise innovation, and technical precision. Used for primary CTAs, active status, focused borders, and brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#84CC16` - Vivid Lime):** Signifies renewal, sustainability, and qualitative transformation. Used for impact indicators, high-trust highlights, conversion flourishes, and metric callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#0F172A` - Executive Slate):** Heavy institutional tone. Used for deep-contrast headers, executive summary cards, and premium navigation elements.
- **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Muted):** Neutral base for secondary copy, metadata, low-emphasis icons, and structural divider lines.

### Surface System
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Off-slate white) providing crisp contrast against card surfaces.
- **Card/Container Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White).
- **Subtle Stroke/Border:** `#E2E8F0` for crisp architectural separation.
- **Gradient Accents:** Linear 135deg gradient shifting from `#84CC16` through `#0EA5E9` to `#0284C7` used sparingly across metric badges, hero pill tags, and key visual dividers.

## Typography

The pairing blends the editorial gravitas of **Playfair Display** with the crisp geometric humanism of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Playfair Display:** Reserved exclusively for display headlines, thought leadership narratives, hero titles, and major stat callouts. It evokes the prestige of top-tier publication journals and management consulting briefs.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Governs operational interfaces, subheadings, dense data tables, interactive components, labels, and running body text. Its tall x-height and open apertures maintain pristine legibility across high-density desktop dashboards and handheld screens alike.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 8pt spatial baseline maintains rhythm across consulting frameworks, case study cards, and metric grids.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column layout with `3rem` section margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Max-width content boundary is capped at `1400px` for optimal reading scan lines.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` section margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.

Vertical section padding alternates between `space-xl` (40px) and `space-xl * 2` (80px) to give intellectual breathing room between distinct service offerings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy combines clean editorial borders with layered frosted glass accents.

### Depth Archetypes
1. **Resting Flat Cards:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a fine 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with no drop shadow, creating a razor-sharp corporate appearance.
2. **Elevated Advisory Cards (Hover / Active):**
   - Box Shadow: `0 12px 30px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.04)`
   - Border transitions from `#E2E8F0` to a subtle sky tint `#BAE6FD`.
3. **Glassmorphic Floating Panels (Sticky Nav, Floating Case Metrics):**
   - Background: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(180%)`
   - Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)` with an inner ambient glow `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.
4. **Hero Accent Gradients:** Subtly glowing backdrops using ambient mesh blurs of `#84CC16` and `#38BDF8` set at `12%` opacity behind key strategic charts.

## Shapes

The geometry strikes a balance between corporate rigor and ergonomic modernity. A level of **2 (Rounded)** establishes tailored soft corners that mirror the smooth organic curves of the reference logo's leaf tips.

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** `0.5rem` (`rounded`) to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Feature Banners & Modals:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`).
- **Badges, Filter Chips & Primary Buttons:** Fully rounded pill format (`rounded-full` or 9999px) to echo the dynamic circular and leaf nodes of the brand emblem.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid `#0284C7` background, white label typography, pill-shaped radius. Features a subtle dynamic hover highlight: transitions to `#0369A1` accompanied by a directional soft cyan glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary / Eco-Transformation CTA:** Vibrant lime accent background (`#84CC16`) with dark slate typography (`#0F172A`) for high-contrast visibility.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1.5px solid `#CBD5E1` border, text `#0F172A`. Transitions on hover to `#F1F5F9` background with `#0284C7` text.

### High-Trust Badges & Metric Chips
- **ESG & Compliance Badges:** Pill-shaped, semi-transparent frosted background (`rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.12)`), text color `#4D7C0F`, adorned with a micro 6px status dot.
- **Strategic Category Chips:** Background `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, typography `label-sm` in `#1E293B`. When active, fills with `#0F172A` background and crisp `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards & Case Modules
- **Standard Service Card:** White background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `1rem` corner radius, `space-lg` internal padding. Top edge features an optional 3px hairline gradient indicator running from lime (`#84CC16`) to azure (`#0284C7`).
- **Executive Summary Card:** Dark theme variant using `#0F172A` background with crisp white display headlines, providing a grounding focal point on data dashboards.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Base height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, border radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus State: 1.5px border `#0284C7` paired with an external ring glow `0 0 0 4px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)`.
- Helper and error text rendered in `body-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp 18px dimensions, 1.5px border `#94A3B8`, rounded `4px` (checkbox) or circular (radio).
- Active state fills with `#0284C7` containing a white verification checkmark.
